# Phase 1 sentence set for the phonemizer sensitivity measurement.
# Chosen so every espeak feature under test appears in SEVERAL sentences: the alveolar flap
# (water, letter, little, butter, sitting), the barred-i reduced vowel (roses, photographs),
# turned-a (about), r-coloured schwa (mother, brother, better, counter), secondary stress
# (understand, seventeen, photographs) and length marks (all over).
# Everyday vocabulary on purpose: a phonemizer that only works on lab sentences is not a phonemizer.
About the roses, understand that my mother would rather water the better city garden.
My daughter waited by the water for a letter from her brother.
The little kitten sat under the ladder in the middle of the city.
Understanding the problem is better than pretending it never happened.
Seventeen photographs were sitting in a bottle on the kitchen counter.
The birch canoe slid on the smooth planks.
Rice is often served in round bowls with a little butter.
The waitresses collected the boxes and watched the horses.
About a hundred packages arrived at the address about noon.
